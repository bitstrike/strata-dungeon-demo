Dungeon Torchlightfirst person · webassembly · webgl2

A lamp-borne crawl through a stone maze. Your torch reaches three squares; everything past that pool is dark. Sconces are cut into the walls every six blocks and each throws light two squares into the corridor. The view follows the reach of your flame and nearby sconces, which is why an 80×80 maze costs the same as a 6×6 one.
